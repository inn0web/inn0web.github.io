A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Let’s make
it clear.

Do you sell these four bots as packages?+

Each system is built around your workflow. The four examples show the kinds of triggers, decisions, integrations and human controls we can design.

Can a person approve an action?+

Yes. Approvals can sit before a reply, order, data change or other important action. We define what the system can do on its own and what needs review.

Which tools can you connect?+

We start with the APIs, webhooks and integrations your tools support. Authorised browser automation can be considered when suitable. Access and platform rules are assessed during scoping.

What happens when something fails?+

We design timeouts, duplicate prevention, bounded retries, alerts and human handover. The right recovery path depends on the action and its risk.

Can you guarantee a checkout or bypass CAPTCHA?+

No. Purchasing depends on stock, approvals, payment providers and platform restrictions. CAPTCHA or access challenges are handed to an authorised person.

How long does a project take?+

We confirm a schedule after mapping the workflow, integrations and approval points. A focused prototype comes first, followed by the agreed build and testing scope.
