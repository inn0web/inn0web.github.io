A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Let’s make
it clear.

What does inn0web build?+

Custom bots and automation systems, websites and landing pages, and Shopify stores. Each project is designed around your workflow, your tools and your customers.

How much does a project cost?+

Projects start from $120. Nothing is fixed-price: we look at the workflow or the pages you need, then quote for that scope before any work begins.

Where are you based?+

inn0web is based in the United Kingdom and works with clients remotely, wherever they are.

How do we start?+

Message us on WhatsApp or send a short brief. We map what you need, agree the scope and the price, and make the important parts tangible before the full build.

Can a person approve what a bot does?+

Yes. Approvals can sit before a reply, order, data change or other important action. We define what the system can do on its own and what needs review.

Which tools can you connect?+

We start with the APIs, webhooks and integrations your tools support. Access and platform rules are assessed during scoping.
