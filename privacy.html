Privacy notice

This notice explains what happens to your information when you use inn0web.com. The site is operated by inn0web, United Kingdom.

Project enquiries

The project form does not send or store anything on this website. When you choose “Send on WhatsApp” or “Send by email”, your brief is turned into a message that opens in your own WhatsApp or email app, and it is only sent when you press send there. Please do not include passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information.

What we keep

Messages you send us are kept in WhatsApp or in our email inbox so that we can discuss your project. If you become a client, any further project records and terms are agreed with you directly. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

Preferences and analytics

Your motion preference may be stored in your browser. The site does not set advertising cookies and does not send behavioural analytics to a third-party analytics service. The hosting provider processes technical request logs, such as IP address and browser type, that are needed to serve and secure the site.

External services

Links to WhatsApp, Instagram and email open services with their own privacy practices. Product demonstration recordings on this site are served from inn0web.com and are credited to their publishers; watching them does not contact those publishers.

Contact and requests

To ask about your information or request removal, email hello@inn0web.com.
